About ICHRA

ICHRA (Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement) is a health benefit model where employers provide a fixed, tax-free monthly allowance for employees to purchase their own individual health insurance plans on the open market.

Unlike traditional group health insurance where the employer selects one plan for everyone, ICHRA gives employees the freedom to choose plans that fit their unique needs while giving employers predictable, controlled costs.

Traditional group insurance uses a defined benefit model — the employer selects one plan (or a few options) for all employees.

ICHRA uses a defined contribution model — the employer sets a monthly allowance and each employee chooses their own individual plan. This means more flexibility, more personalization, and more cost predictability for both sides.

ICHRA has no minimum or maximum employer size requirement. Any employer — from a 1-person company to a Fortune 500 corporation — can offer an ICHRA. It is especially popular with small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs) that want cost-predictable health benefits without the complexity of traditional group plans.

Yes. ICHRA was officially established by a federal rule in June 2019 and is fully compliant with the Affordable Care Act (ACA). Employers who offer an ICHRA that meets affordability requirements satisfy the ACA employer mandate.
