The Industry Is Lying To You.

01

"ICHRA is too complicated to do yourself."

They tell you it's impossibly complex so you'll hand over the reins. The truth? With the right platform, modeling and enrolling an ICHRA is a highly streamlined, repeatable process that you can own entirely.

02

"You need a TPA to handle quoting."

This is how they get between you and your client. If they control the quote, they control the relationship. A true agent platform gives you the live quoting engine directly, cutting out the middleman entirely.

03

"A Broker of Record letter is required."

This is the ultimate trap. When you sign over the BOR, you surrender your commissions and your client list. You should never have to sacrifice your AOR just to provide an ICHRA solution to a client.

How These Lies Cost You Real Money

$

Lost Commissions

Every client you route through a third-party platform is a client whose commissions are at risk. When they control the AOR, they decide who gets paid — and how much. Agents across the country have reported commission reductions of 30-50% after signing BOR letters with third-party ICHRA providers.

⏳

Slower Sales Cycles

When you can't quote in real time, your sales cycle stretches from minutes to days. Your client gets impatient, shops around, or — worst case — the employer goes directly to the platform that quoted them. You lose the deal because someone else controlled the speed.

🔗

Platform Lock-In

Once a third-party platform holds your client data, your quoting history, and your AOR, leaving becomes nearly impossible. They know this. That's why they make the onboarding easy and the exit painful. You become dependent on a system that was designed to replace you.

How to Evaluate Any ICHRA Platform

Instead of relying on positioning or promises, evaluate based on control. If you can't answer these questions clearly, that's your red flag.

Who controls the quote?

If you can't quote in real time with your client, someone else is shaping the sale.

Is AOR required?

If a platform asks for your Agent of Record, they're positioning to replace you.

Who communicates with the client?

If they talk to your client, they control the relationship. Your role starts to shrink.

Where does commission flow?

Your book isn't just current income — it's future and renewal income. Trace every dollar.

Who owns the renewal?

If they handle renewals, they control the future revenue. That's your long-term value.

Who controls enrollment?

If you're locked out of enrollment, you're locked out of the client lifecycle entirely.
